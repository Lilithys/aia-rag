### What will happen if I don't respond to the NOTICE OF NON-COMPLIANCE AND LICENSE DOWNGRADE?

Failure to respond to the NOTICE OF NON-COMPLIANCE AND LICENSE DOWNGRADE will result in your Commercial Driver License automatically being downgraded to a Non-CDL class and/or your Commercial Learner Permit being cancelled as of the effective date on the notice. We strongly recommend that you submit your required information to the Medical Certification Unit at least 20 days before the effective date of the downgrade. This will allow for receipt and processing time.

If the downgrade/cancellation occurs, your must complete and present an application to reinstate your commercial privileges in person at a DMV office. All normal transaction fees will apply. If you are a Commercial Learner Permit holder, you will need to retake any applicable knowledge tests you passed more than 180 days from the date you apply for reinstatement.

If you no longer want a Commercial Driver License/ Commercial Learner Permit, you can get a non-commercial duplicate license online \[4\] after the Effective Date on the downgrade notice. The commercial class license document in your possession will no longer be valid.

Note: You may be charged with a violation of the Vehicle and Traffic Law (VTL) if you operate a commercial motor vehicle when

- not medically certified (VTL 509 (7-a))
- not in possession of the appropriate license to operate the vehicle (VTL 509(7), or
- not holding the appropriate class of license to operate the vehicle VTL 509(2)

Sections 509(2) and 509(7) are considered serious disqualifying offenses under federal and state law, when committed in a commercial motor vehicle. If you are convicted of more than one serious , you commercial driver license may be suspended.
